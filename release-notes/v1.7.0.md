Rico has a proper voice now.

- New expressive voice that runs entirely on your PC: a cocky little cartoon sidekick who can chuckle, sigh, groan and gasp. It uses your graphics card (AMD, NVIDIA or Intel) and starts talking while the rest of the line is still being made
- Get it from the button after updating, or Settings > Voice > Download Rico's voice (2 GB, one-off)
- Subtle hologram shimmer on his voice, adjustable in Settings > Voice, along with his cartoon pitch
- His mouth now moves with the actual sound of his voice
- Mood-based delivery: quicker and brighter when he's happy, drier when he's smug, a groan when he's annoyed
- Glitchy, crackly voice when something goes wrong
- Little noises: hmms, heh, pfft, oi, whoa, yawns and a ta-da
- In games he switches to the Windows voice and unloads his real one after a minute, so you don't lose frames
