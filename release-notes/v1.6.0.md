Rico's biggest update yet.

- New look: Rico is now a 3D hologram projected from a glowing emitter puck. He turns to follow your cursor, gestures while he talks, spins when he's bored and glitches now and then
- Stays out of your way: fades away when your cursor comes near (hover still to bring him back), shrinks when idle, tucks into a glowing corner tab, and hides during fullscreen games and videos (reminders are spoken instead)
- Local-only mode (on by default): nothing about you leaves your PC. Chat uses his offline brain; Claude and web lookups are off unless you turn it off in Settings > Chat
- Print Shop: instant quotes from a dropped .3mf/.gcode or "price 120g of PETG, 5 hours"; filament, electricity, machine wear, failure buffer, your time, packaging, add-ons, Royal Mail postage and selling fees; three price tiers with real profit; product catalogue; log sales to Money
- 3D printer: watches your Bambu H2D on the local network (read-only), shows progress on his puck, and reacts when a print finishes, fails or needs you; maintenance reminders by print hours; offline troubleshooting ("my print is stringing"); "find me a model of..." opens MakerWorld, Printables and Thingiverse
- Money (100% local): budget by category, bank CSV import with auto-categorising, bills and subscription reminders, "spent £12 on lunch", "how much left for groceries?"
- Game help: spots the game you're playing, hints before spoilers, checklists for collectibles, and occasional spoken tips
- More personality: lots of new lines, smarter app tips for art and UX work
